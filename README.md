# SUCI Constructions

Official website for **SUCI Constructions** — an engineer-led construction company based in Hyderabad, building villas, independent houses, and commercial buildings across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

🌐 **Website:** [suciconstructions.com](https://www.suciconstructions.com/)

---

## 🏗️ Overview

SUCI Constructions provides transparent, turnkey construction services with zero hidden costs, engineered structural quality, and live milestone tracking.

### Key Pages & Features
- **Home (`index.html`)**: Company highlights, structural comparison viewer, core packages, and free site consultation booking.
- **Projects (`projects.html`)**: Portfolio of completed and ongoing residential and commercial construction projects with filterable gallery.
- **Pricing (`pricing.html`)**: Transparent pricing tiers starting from ₹2,090/sq ft (incl. GST) with cost estimator calculator.
- **Services (`services.html`)**: Comprehensive services covering architectural design, structural engineering, turnkey construction, and approvals.
- **About (`about.html`)**: Team, engineering leadership, quality standards, and company mission.
- **Contact (`contact.html`)**: Consultation booking, office locations, and direct inquiry forms.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5 & Vanilla CSS3**: Semantic markup, responsive design, custom variables, and micro-interactions.
- **Vanilla JavaScript**: Dynamic project filtering, cost calculator, comparison slider, and mobile navigation.
- **SEO & Performance**: JSON-LD Schema markup, XML sitemap, robots.txt, and optimized WebP media assets.
